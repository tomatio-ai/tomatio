Impact score: the item's place on the ranked list on a 100-point scale (100 = top of Now); Inbox items are not ranked yet. Effort: 1 to 100, from the estimated agent hours, tokens and decisions a person must make. Priority: the impact score divided by the effort; it has no override, so to change it, fix the estimate or the rank. Urgent items, marked by a project admin, come first in the Priority sort. The rank and the release say when. Release: the board opens on the next release (the lowest planned version after the one live now); the tabs above it show another release, Later, the Inbox or All releases, and a search looks in every release. Click a row to mark it Done, Not needed or Keep and to comment: everyone who can open this page sees the same notes, and your AI reads them with the project tools. All shows open work only: an item marked Done stays in its release, struck through and not ranked, until that release ships and it moves to Shipped; what is not needed, or a rejected idea, moves to the Archive, where anyone can restore it. Nothing disappears, and a search looks in every view: planned work and ideas first, then what was done, decided or shipped, folded away. Ideas are not work yet: log one with + Idea (it lands in the Inbox), comment on it, and a curator promotes it to an item or rejects it.

Rank and change items through your AI (the project tools). On Tomagic, members mark items and comment.
