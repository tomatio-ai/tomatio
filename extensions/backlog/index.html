How Tomagic works

One list, ranked once. View shows it by Priority (the Inbox, new and not ranked yet, then Now, Next and Later), by Releases (a tab per release, the next one first, then Not scheduled and No release; All shows them all), as a Roadmap (every release as a column: drag a card to another release, or use its release menu; only the release changes, its rank stays; shipped releases take no drop) or by Theme (the area). Shipped has a tab per shipped release, newest first, so you see what went out in each. Impact (the place on the list, 100 = top of Now), Effort (1 to 100, from the estimated agent hours, tokens and decisions) and Priority (impact divided by effort; urgent items first) show on every row; Show scores under Filters turns them off. Sort sits next to the search: by rank, priority, least effort, comments, name or assigned, ↓/↑ to flip; the column heads sort too. Your view and the scores switch are remembered. Share view copies a link to what you see. Done and Decided list what your TíoDay recorded in its last 30 days. The chips are the filters, so a count always matches the list under it. Click a row to open it: set its release and who it is assigned to, mark it Done, Not needed or Keep, and comment; everyone who can open this page sees the same notes, and your AI reads them with the project tools. Done work stays in its release, struck through, until the release ships and it moves to Shipped; what is not needed, or a rejected idea, moves to the Archive, where anyone can restore it. + Add logs an idea; a curator makes it an item or rejects it.
